Godavari Pushkaralu

Prepare for a pilgrimage connected with the sacred Godavari river and the Kaleshwaram region.

What are Pushkaralu?

Godavari Pushkaralu is a major river-based Hindu pilgrimage observance traditionally associated with the movement of Jupiter into a zodiac sign. Devotees visit designated river locations for ritual bathing, prayer and charitable activities.

Dates, bathing ghats, traffic plans, crowd-management arrangements and permitted activities are announced by the relevant authorities for each Pushkaram period.

Plan ahead
  • Check official dates and government announcements.
  • Use designated ghats only.
  • Follow crowd and traffic instructions.
  • Book accommodation through verified providers.
  • Carry essential medicines and drinking water.
