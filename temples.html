Sri Kaleshwara Mukteshwara Swamy Temple

The Sri Kaleshwara Mukteshwara Swamy Temple is the central pilgrimage landmark of Kaleshwaram in Jayashankar Bhupalpally district, Telangana. The shrine is widely associated with the worship of Lord Shiva and the distinctive tradition of two Shiva lingas in the sanctum.

Devotees visit for darshan and customary worship. Specific seva availability, ticket rates, timings and festival arrangements are determined by the temple administration and may change.

Before travel, confirm current darshan timings, pooja booking rules, dress guidance and special-day arrangements with the temple office.
Visitor checklist
  • Confirm temple opening and closing times.
  • Check current pooja and darshan procedures.
  • Allow extra time on weekends and festival days.
  • Use authorised parking and local transport.
  • Follow temple and river-safety instructions.
